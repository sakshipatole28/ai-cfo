## Monthly portfolio disclosure
Asset management companies disclose the full portfolio of each scheme as on the last day of every month. The disclosure is published on the fund house's website and on the AMFI website within 10 days of the month end, in a downloadable spreadsheet format that lists each security with its ISIN and its percentage of net assets.

## Using disclosures for look-through analysis
Because every scheme's holdings are public each month, an investor's true exposure to an individual company can be calculated by multiplying the value held in each fund by that fund's weight in the company and adding any shares held directly. The overlap between two funds can be measured by adding the smaller of the two weights for every company both funds hold. These figures are only as recent as the latest monthly disclosure.

## Riskometer
Every scheme carries a riskometer with six levels: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High and Very High. The risk level is evaluated every month from the scheme's portfolio and any change is disclosed. Advisers compare the riskometer with the client's risk profile when judging suitability.

## Expense ratio disclosure
The total expense ratio of each scheme and plan is disclosed on the fund house's website and changes are notified in advance. Direct plans have lower expense ratios than regular plans because they carry no distribution commission.
