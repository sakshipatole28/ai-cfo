## How the framework works
An Account Aggregator is an RBI-licensed entity that moves financial data from a Financial Information Provider (FIP), such as a bank, depository or registrar, to a Financial Information User (FIU), such as a lender or a SEBI-registered adviser, only after the customer approves a consent request. The Account Aggregator cannot read or store the data it carries.

## The consent artefact
Every data request is described by a consent artefact that states the purpose, the types of data, the period covered, how often data may be fetched and how long the consent lasts. The customer can approve, reject, pause or revoke a consent from the Account Aggregator app at any time.

## Securities and mutual fund data
SEBI allowed depositories to join the framework as Financial Information Providers in August 2022, so demat holdings from NSDL and CDSL can be shared with consent. Mutual fund registrars such as CAMS and KFintech also act as providers for mutual fund folios. A Financial Information User must itself be a regulated entity, so an unregulated software provider can access this data only on behalf of a regulated customer.
