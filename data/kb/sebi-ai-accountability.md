## Who is responsible for AI output
A SEBI-regulated entity that uses artificial intelligence or machine learning tools, whether built in-house or bought from a third-party technology provider, is solely responsible for the privacy, security and integrity of investor data used by those tools, for the output they produce and for compliance with applicable laws. Outsourcing a tool does not move this responsibility to the vendor.

## What regulated entities should expect from vendors
Because the regulated entity remains accountable, it will ask technology providers for evidence of data protection, testing of model outputs, records that explain how each output was produced, and the ability to switch AI features off. Human oversight of AI output before it reaches investors reduces the risk of unsuitable or misleading communication.

## Proposed guidelines
A June 2025 consultation paper proposed principles for responsible use of AI in the securities market, including governance and accountability, testing and monitoring of models, data privacy and security, fairness, and disclosure to investors where AI is used in client-facing services, with lighter requirements for purely internal uses.
