## Who must register
A person who gives investment advice for consideration must be registered with SEBI as an investment adviser, unless an exemption applies. Investment advice covers advice on investing in, buying, selling or otherwise dealing in securities or investment products, including financial planning.

## Risk profiling and suitability
A registered investment adviser must assess each client's risk profile using information about the client's finances, goals and capacity for loss, and must ensure that any advice is suitable for that risk profile. Advice should be consistent with the client's agreed asset allocation.

## Records of advice
Investment advisers must keep records that include client KYC, the risk profiling and suitability assessment, the advice given and the rationale for arriving at that advice. Records are to be maintained for at least five years. A platform that stores the inputs, calculations and the adviser's rationale for every recommendation supports this duty.

## Separating advice from distribution
An investment adviser may not provide distribution services to its own advisory clients. Advice and distribution must be kept separate at the client level, with the family treated as a single unit for this purpose.

## Use of artificial intelligence tools
An investment adviser that uses artificial intelligence tools in its advisory services is solely responsible for the security, confidentiality and integrity of client data and for the advice produced, and must disclose to clients the extent to which such tools are used.
